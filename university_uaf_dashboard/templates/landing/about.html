About UAF

The University of Agriculture Faisalabad (UAF) is one of the oldest and most prestigious agricultural universities in Pakistan, established in 1906. It is a leading institution in agricultural education, research, and extension.

Our Mission

To advance knowledge through quality education, innovative research, and community engagement in agriculture and allied disciplines for sustainable development.
