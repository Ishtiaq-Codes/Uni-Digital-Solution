Platform Features

Everything you need for modern university management.

📚 Course Management

Create courses, upload materials (PDF, PPT, DOCX), create assignments with deadlines, and manage enrollments.

💳 Fee Voucher System

Generate fee vouchers, upload payment receipts, admin verification with approve/reject workflow.

📄 UG/PG Form Management

Submit admission, scholarship, exam forms (UG) and GS-10 forms (PG) with multi-level approval tracking.

📊 QEC Feedback

Anonymous course & faculty ratings with analytics dashboard and Chart.js visualizations.

🔔 Smart Notifications

Real-time in-app notifications for announcements, fee deadlines, form status changes, and more.

📈 Reports & Analytics

Data-driven dashboards with exportable CSV reports for students, fees, and QEC feedback.

🔐 Role-Based Access

7 distinct user roles with separate dashboards, permissions, and secure access control.

🔍 Global Search

Search across students, faculty, courses, departments, and announcements from one search bar.
